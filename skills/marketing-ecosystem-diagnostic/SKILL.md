# Marketing ecosystem diagnostic

## Purpose

Marketing reviews often list what the company does, not how well it does it. A channel can be "running" with no cadence and no measurement. This diagnostic grades each motion on evidence, shows where the gaps are, and turns them into a short list of next moves, so the roadmap comes from the gaps.

## When to use

- Someone asks where marketing spend should go.
- A channel is claimed to work, and the claim needs checking.
- A marketing plan needs a baseline before it is changed.

Route elsewhere when:

- The question is a single paid campaign. Use `paid-campaign-plan`.
- The question is the revenue process as a whole. Use `move-gtm-diagnostic`.

## Inputs

- Monthly spend by channel, from the company's records.
- The motions the company runs, and any evidence of cadence and measurement.
- Audience sizes: retargeting pools, contact records, and subscriber lists.

### The motions

Grade each of the 22 motions from L0 to L3:

- L0: not running.
- L1: ad hoc, unmeasured.
- L2: consistent and measured.
- L3: systematic, compounding, and optimized.

**Create (demand and awareness):** organic search content cadence; thought leadership and posting cadence; owned audience such as a newsletter; video, podcast, or community; brand and awareness paid activity.

**Capture (in-market demand):** paid search structure with negatives; search landing-page coverage; review and listing presence; retargeting of site visitors and engagers; lead offers beyond a demo request.

**Connect (signal and data):** conversion tracking into the CRM; visitor identification and routing; account-level engagement data reviewed; enrichment owned by a named person; matched-audience uploads managed.

**Convert (pipeline):** full-funnel structure separating awareness, consideration, and decision; named-account motion; landing pages per offer; outbound sequences live; hot-account routing to sales.

**Compound (measurement and iteration):** a reporting cadence with attribution; structured experiments.

## Procedure

1. **Grade each motion** on evidence. A motion claimed without cadence or measurement is L1 until shown otherwise. Attach an owner tag: internal, agency, partner, or none.
2. **Compute the maturity score** as the average grade across all 22 motions, and show the grade distribution.
3. **Map spend to funnel stage** from the company's records. Show where spend sits against where the gaps are.
4. **Check the audience ladder.** Are warm pools growing from awareness through to contact? A small or shrinking warm pool is a finding.
5. **Name exactly three next moves.** Each names the motion, the change, the owner, and the measure that will show it worked. Choose the moves with the most leverage for the stage, not the easiest.

## Output

- The grade table for all 22 motions, with the evidence and owner tag for each.
- The maturity score and the grade distribution.
- The spend-by-funnel view, from the company's own figures.
- Three next moves, with owner and measure.

## Quality bar

Each grade cites evidence. No spend figure appears without a source. Exactly three next moves are named. Watch for grades given on claims, motions marked L2 without a measure, and a recommendation list longer than three.
