# SEO account context

## Purpose

SEO research that starts from a keyword tool produces a list of searches, not a plan for the business. The plan depends on understanding what the company sells, to whom, and what already works. This skill builds that understanding first, as a document the team confirms before any research begins.

## When to use

- A new company is starting SEO or content work.
- A content plan exists, but nobody can say who it is for.
- Keyword research is about to start, and the account picture is thin.

Route elsewhere when:
- The question is which topics to write about. Use `seo-topic-research`.
- The question is the account's full commercial profile for sales. Use `account-research-brief`.

## Inputs

Ask once, in one message, for whatever is available:
- the SEO onboarding form, if one exists,
- the company's website, including its product, pricing, and key landing pages,
- sales call notes or transcripts that show how customers describe the problem,
- client communications that show priorities and objections,
- any existing content performance data.

Gather everything in one pass. Do not ask for the same thing twice.

## Procedure

### The account intelligence document

Write one document with these sections:

1. **What they sell.** Products, the problem each solves, and the outcome customers get, in the company's words and the customers' words where they differ.
2. **Who they sell to.** Buyer roles, company types, and the buying group.
3. **What they want to sell more of.** The products, segments, or outcomes that matter most now.
4. **What already works.** Pages, topics, and channels that bring in the right visitors or deals.
5. **What is unclear.** Facts that could not be confirmed, and the question to ask the company.

Mark every claim as **confirmed** (from the company or its data) or **unverified** (inferred from the website or elsewhere).

### Steps

1. **Gather the inputs** in one request.
2. **Draft the document** using the five sections.
3. **Mark each claim** as confirmed or unverified.
4. **Send the document for confirmation,** with the unclear items listed as questions.
5. **Do not start keyword work** until the company has confirmed the sections on what they sell, who they sell to, and what they want to sell more of.

## Output

- The account intelligence document, with each claim marked.
- The list of unclear items, as questions for the company.
- A confirmation request.

## Quality bar

Every claim is confirmed or marked unverified. The priorities section comes from the company, not the team's guess. Keyword work does not start before confirmation. Watch for a document built only from the website, priorities inferred from competitors, and a research plan started before the buyer is confirmed.
