## What we do

GTM-360 is a B2B go-to-market advisory and consulting practice. The focus is AI-native and agentic revenue systems, including Salesforce Agentforce implementations, RevOps, and GTM strategy for B2B SaaS companies.

## Framework

Organized around customer outcomes rather than functions:

1. **Generate demand**
2. **Win customers**
3. **Delight**
4. **Expand**

Each outcome is led by a cross-functional pod, rather than a single functional head such as a sales head.
